Who We Are

A Legacy of Compassion & Excellence

Khidmat-e-Alam Welfare Trust is a non-profit humanitarian organization founded in 2005 with a sincere passion to serve humanity. We have grown into a comprehensive welfare and healthcare ecosystem serving thousands of lives across Karachi and beyond.

Under the leadership of Chairman Mr. Himayatullah Sadukhani, we operate a full-service Medical Centre, community welfare programs, water supply projects, and regular outreach camps — all built on the principles of transparency, equity, and compassionate care.

What We Offer
Comprehensive Medical & Welfare Services

Khidmat-e-Alam Medical Centre provides round-the-clock care through world-class facilities and deeply committed professionals.

01

Outpatient Clinics (OPD)

Multi-specialty clinics with Child Specialists, Neurologists, Gastroenterologists, Dermatologists, Gynecologists, Hepatologists, and Diabetologists delivering 500+ daily consultations.

02

24-Hour Urgent Care Unit

Immediate emergency response with experienced physicians, nurses, and paramedical teams equipped with life-saving technology for round-the-clock critical care.

03

Advanced Diagnostic Centre

Complete diagnostics: Laboratory, Ultrasound, Digital X-Ray, and ECG — 1,000+ tests monthly with precision-driven results supporting better clinical decisions.

04

Fully Equipped Pharmacy

Wide-range authentic medicines dispensed by qualified pharmacists with strict quality standards, extended hours, and direct doctor-pharmacist coordination.

05

24/7 Ambulance Service

Medical-grade transport with trained paramedic staff ensuring safe, rapid patient transfer — from doorstep to treatment facility with continuous care.

06

Free Medical Camps

Regular outreach camps serving underserved communities: Sugar checks, Eye camps, Malaria screening, Medicine distribution, Rehabilitation, and First Aid training.

Welfare Financial Aid System

Structured 3-tier support: 25% initial assistance → 50% Zakat-funded support → 100% full coverage for those unable to pay. All cases documented per PCP requirements.

Water Supply Projects

Public Sabeel drinking facility · RO Plant certified by Aga Khan Lab & PCSIR · Two groundwater bore wells serving 10,000+ community members annually.

Free Medical Equipment

Oxygen cylinders, wheelchairs, nebulizers, BP apparatus, glucometers, crutches, walkers, stethoscopes, and drip stands provided free to critical-need patients.
